# Governance

SkyWatch funds and maintains the project. Editorial credibility depends on transparent authority boundaries and external review.

## Roles

- **Maintainers:** releases, security, repository operations, source-health enforcement.
- **Domain reviewers:** approve content inside their credentials and experience.
- **Contributors:** propose corrections, tools, sources, and lessons.
- **Users:** report ambiguity, staleness, unsafe behavior, or missing escalation.

## Rules

1. Safety-critical changes require two approvals: one domain reviewer and one maintainer.
2. Commercial sponsors cannot buy inclusion, ranking, or educational conclusions.
3. Contributors disclose relevant financial interests.
4. Regulations, official guidance, common practice, and opinion remain visibly distinct.
5. Corrections are public, dated, and preserved in version history.
6. Stale safety-critical material is labeled or withdrawn from confident responses.
7. SkyWatch product references are optional, factual, relevant, and clearly commercial.

The long-term target is a 5–7 person steering group with at least half of seats held by external aviation experts.
