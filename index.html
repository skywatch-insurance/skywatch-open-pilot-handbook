See the difference

The same AI. A much better aviation answer.

SkyWatch Co-Pilot does not turn AI into an encyclopedia. It turns broad questions into stage-appropriate plans with exact resources, visible assumptions, readiness gates, and clear verification steps.

01 · Training plan

Turn “study more” into the right next four weeks

“I’m a 28-hour private-pilot student struggling with VFR cross-country planning and radio confidence. Build my next four weeks using only free resources. Give me exact links, readiness gates, and what to postpone.”

Without the skill

A broad ground-school checklist, generic resources, and no clear exit criteria.

With SkyWatch Co-Pilot

A focused sequence for the pilot’s stage, credited lessons, instructor-review points, measurable readiness gates, and lower-priority subjects deliberately deferred.

02 · Exact resources

Replace plausible recommendations with cataloged sources

“Give me the exact free video lessons and free FAA courses that cover VFR cross-country nav-log construction. Links, not descriptions.”

Without the skill

General playlists and broad handbook suggestions that still require manual verification.

With SkyWatch Co-Pilot

Mapped Free Pilot Training lessons, FAASTeam course ALC-481, original-publisher credit, and cataloged links instead of memory-based guesses.

03 · Auditable tools

See every assumption behind the answer

“Show a complete weight-and-balance calculation with every station, moment, total, CG, unit, and assumption. Then tell me which aircraft-specific documents I must verify.”

Without the skill

Prose arithmetic that can blur a teaching estimate with an aircraft-specific conclusion.

With SkyWatch Co-Pilot

Transparent calculator output, visible units and assumptions, and a precise verification step using the aircraft’s current approved records.

How it works?

The skill does not dump an aviation encyclopedia into every conversation. It identifies the task, loads only the relevant references and tools, and applies explicit verification and professional-review boundaries.

01 · Classify

Understand the pilot

Identifies training stage, jurisdiction, aircraft context, and whether the request is education, planning, live operations, or professional advice.

02 · Retrieve

Load what matters

Routes to the right curriculum module, ownership journey, checklist, calculator, playlist, or source record.

03 · Ground

Use authority first

Prefers current FAA, eCFR, NTSB, NOAA/NWS, and approved aircraft documents over creator content or model memory.

04 · Escalate

Know the boundary

Points to a CFI, A&P/IA, AME, attorney, CPA, title professional, or licensed insurance representative when judgment is required.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a complete, approved ground school?

No. It is an open learning and decision-support layer aligned to authoritative sources. It does not issue graduation certificates, endorsements, or replace an instructor or approved course.

How do I install it in Claude?

Open the GitHub repository and download or clone it. Package only the skywatch-open-pilot-handbook folder as a ZIP, keeping SKILL.md directly inside that single top-level folder, then upload it in Claude’s skill area.

Does it include Free Pilot Training transcripts?

The transcripts were used to understand topic sequencing and learning objectives, but are not republished. The repository links to the original videos and records them as link-only unless broader rights are granted.

Where do the 177 lessons and courses come from?

The collection combines the 64-lesson Free Pilot Training private-pilot foundation with 83 free FAA Safety Team courses and 30 free AOPA courses listed in FAA Safety.gov. Specialty courses are clearly cataloged and are not automatically treated as core fixed-wing training.

Can the skill tell me whether a flight is safe?

No. It can teach a decision framework and help you find current sources, but it cannot make live go/no-go, weather, NOTAM, airworthiness, or emergency-authority decisions.

Can it interpret my insurance policy or recommend tax and legal decisions?

No. It can explain general concepts and prepare questions, but the actual policy and licensed insurance review control coverage. Legal and tax actions require qualified professionals familiar with your facts and jurisdiction.

Who can contribute?

Everyone is welcome to suggest improvements. Pilots, CFIs/CFIIs, aircraft owners, A&P/IAs, AMEs, DPEs, creators, and aviation organizations can open issues or pull requests. Contributions need original-source credit, and safety-critical changes require domain and maintainer review.

What is included in the GitHub repository?

The repository includes the portable skill, curriculum and source maps, practical tools, governance, GitHub workflows, rights ledger, contribution documentation, and the website source.
