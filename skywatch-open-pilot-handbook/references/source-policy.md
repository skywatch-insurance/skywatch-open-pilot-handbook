# Source and content policy

## Hierarchy

1. **Controlling/current primary:** eCFR, FAA, NTSB, NOAA/NWS/AWC, current approved AFM/POH and maintenance data.
2. **Recognized institutional:** AOPA Air Safety Institute, EAA, FAASTeam partners, recognized type clubs and educational institutions.
3. **Qualified expert:** credentialed CFI/CFII, A&P/IA, AME, attorney, CPA, licensed insurance professional; use inside their scope.
4. **Educational media:** creator videos, podcasts, blogs, forums. Use for teaching approaches, questions, and discovery—not as sole authority.

## Consequential claims

A consequential claim could affect safety, legality, certification, maintenance, money, title, medical status, or coverage. It needs:

- source ID and direct URL;
- jurisdiction;
- effective/revision date when available;
- date checked;
- content classification;
- reviewer role;
- a stated uncertainty or escalation point where appropriate.

## Transcript workflow

Creator transcripts may be reviewed to extract:

- lesson sequence;
- learning objectives;
- terminology and common learner questions;
- concepts needing an alternate explanation;
- source gaps to resolve through primary documents.

Do not store or publish full transcripts, lengthy excerpts, screenshots, or close paraphrases unless the rights ledger documents permission or a compatible license. Auto-generated captions may contain material errors and are never authority.

For every transcript-derived lesson:

1. Record the creator, video title, video ID, URL, and review date.
2. Write original learning objectives without copying expression.
3. Verify every consequential claim against current primary sources.
4. Link to the original video.
5. Record rights status as `link-only`, `permission-pending`, `licensed`, or `public-domain`.

## Freshness

- Live operational data: never serve from repository memory.
- Regulations/ACS/FAA guidance: check at answer time when the task depends on current rules.
- Safety-critical knowledge pages: quarterly review.
- General curriculum: semiannual review against the current ACS.
- Ownership estimates: show price date and region; review at least annually.
- Broken or redirected primary sources: weekly automated check.

When a safety-critical source is stale or unavailable, lower confidence and direct the user to the live authority.

## Commercial independence

- Separate education from product calls to action.
- Disclose SkyWatch's maintainer and insurance-provider role.
- Do not rank providers or products based on sponsorship.
- Label commercial links.
- Do not embed affiliate links without a conspicuous disclosure and governance approval.
