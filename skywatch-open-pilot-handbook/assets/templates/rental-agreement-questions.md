# Rental or flying-club agreement questions

- Who may operate the aircraft and under what checkout/currency rules?
- Who is responsible for damage, deductibles, loss of use, towing/recovery, or administrative costs?
- What insurance does the owner/club carry, and what does it not promise the renter?
- Is coverage primary/excess, and is waiver of subrogation addressed?
- What minimum non-owned coverage, if any, is required?
- How are mechanical discrepancies, cancellations, overnight use, fuel, and maintenance handled?
- What events require immediate reporting, and to whom?
- Which documents control when marketing/FAQ language conflicts?
