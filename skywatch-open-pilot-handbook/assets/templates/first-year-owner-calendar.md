# First-year aircraft owner calendar

## First 30 days

- Transition training and conservative operating plan
- Document/record/equipment inventory
- Maintenance contacts and discrepancy workflow
- Inspection/AD/recurring-item calendar with A&P/IA
- Policy/admin changes confirmed with licensed representative
- Baseline budget and reserve

## Monthly

- Reconcile hours, fuel, oil, maintenance, subscriptions, storage, and other spend
- Review squawks with mechanic
- Review proficiency and training needs

## Quarterly

- Mission-fit and personal-minimum review
- Budget vs. actual and reserve adequacy
- Records backup and subscription/equipment review

## 90 days before annual/renewal

- Schedule maintenance and collect records
- Assemble pilot/aircraft/training/utilization/storage/modification changes
- Review questions with A&P/IA and licensed representative
- Plan downtime and cash range

## Year-end

- Planned vs. actual hours, cost, downtime, mission completion, and surprises
- Year-two training, maintenance, reserve, and ownership decision

Dates and requirements must come from current aircraft records, approved data, regulations, and qualified professionals.
