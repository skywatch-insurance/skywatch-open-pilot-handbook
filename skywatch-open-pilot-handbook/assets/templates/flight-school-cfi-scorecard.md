## Ask the school

- Part 61, Part 141, or both—and what changes for this student?
- Typical instructor and aircraft availability at my preferred times?
- Current wet/dry aircraft rate, instructor rate, taxes/fees, and minimums?
- Scheduling/cancellation, maintenance, and aircraft-substitution policies?
- How are stage checks, progress, syllabus, records, and instructor changes handled?
- What insurance or renter-responsibility documents should I review?
- What does a realistic total budget include beyond advertised minimum hours?

## Ask the instructor

- How do you connect ground study to each flight lesson?
- How do you brief, debrief, document progress, and address plateaus?
- What availability and communication expectations should we set?
- What are your expectations for home study and lesson frequency?
- How do you teach risk management and personal minimums?
