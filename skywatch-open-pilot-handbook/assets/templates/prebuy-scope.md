## Records scope

- Identity, registration, title/lien/escrow work handled separately
- Logbook continuity and missing periods
- Annual/100-hour and recurring inspection status
- AD research method and evidence
- Major repairs/alterations and supporting forms/data
- Engine/prop/appliance history and life-limited items
- Weight-and-balance/equipment-list history
- Damage/corrosion disclosure and evidence
- Open discrepancies and deferred items

## Physical inspection scope

Define with the inspector and type expert; include expansion authority, tests, disassembly limits, sampling, engine run/test flight, photos, and discrepancy categories.
