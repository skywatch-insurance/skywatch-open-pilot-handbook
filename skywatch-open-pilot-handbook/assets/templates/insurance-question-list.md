# Aviation insurance question list

## Facts to assemble accurately

- Pilot certificates/ratings, logged experience, recency, training, claims/violations as requested
- Exact aircraft identity, value basis, equipment/modifications, use, base/storage, and expected pilots
- Intended operations and any instruction, rental, club, partnership, or business use

## Ask the licensed representative

- Which policy forms and endorsements apply?
- What are the liability limits and sublimits, if any?
- How is physical damage value handled?
- Who is a named insured, named pilot, or permitted under an open-pilot provision?
- Which uses, territories, pilots, airports, training, or aircraft changes require approval or notice?
- What deductibles, exclusions, warranties, conditions, and claim-reporting duties matter?
- How do owner, renter/non-owned, club, CFI, or hangar exposures interact?
- Can the answer be confirmed in the actual policy/endorsement or in writing?

No worksheet or agent answer determines eligibility, price, coverage, or claim outcome. The actual policy, endorsements, facts, carrier, and licensed review control.
