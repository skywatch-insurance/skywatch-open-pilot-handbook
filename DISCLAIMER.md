# Educational and safety disclaimer

The Open Pilot Handbook provides general educational and planning information for US general aviation. It is not operational authority and is not a substitute for current regulations, NOTAMs, weather products, air traffic control, approved aircraft flight or maintenance documents, training, inspection, professional judgment, or a qualified licensed professional.

No output determines flight safety, legality, airworthiness, medical fitness, tax treatment, legal rights, title, financing, insurance eligibility, price, coverage, or claim outcome. Conditions, laws, regulations, products, and documents change. Verify against current primary sources and the exact aircraft and policy documents before acting.

In an emergency, use trained procedures and the current approved aircraft checklist/AFM/POH, maintain aircraft control, and contact ATC or emergency services as appropriate.
